# Home hero photograph prompts

The home page opens on one wide campaign photograph (Option B below), with the Women, Men and Kids
photographs as a row of tiles under it. Without the campaign photograph, the three panels show side
by side instead. All four photographs made from these prompts are in place (`public/images/hero/`);
use the prompts again to refresh them for a new season.

**Where it goes:** Admin → Settings → **Home page** → Hero → Panel 1 / 2 / 3 → **Photograph** → Upload → **Save changes**.

**Tips**
- Use the same shared style for all three so the panels sit together as one picture.
- Real photographs of your own pieces are always better than generated ones: swap them in as soon as you have them.

**Shared style (already included in every prompt below):**

> Premium high-street fashion campaign photograph (H&M / Zara / COS look). Plain warm beige seamless studio backdrop (#E9E2D6), soft diffused daylight from the left, natural skin tones, true-to-life fabric texture. Framed from the head to just above the knee, the face in the upper third of the frame, the clothes clearly visible, calm natural expression looking at the camera. Leave the bottom fifth of the frame plain (the department name is set there). No text, no logo, no watermark, no props. Portrait 4:5, 2000 x 2500 px.

## Option B: one wide campaign photograph (recommended for the most premium look)

With a campaign photograph set, it fills the whole first screen, the title and buttons sit over its lower left, and the panels become simple Women / Men / Kids links. The panel photographs above are then not needed.

**Where it goes:** Admin → Settings → **Home page** → Hero → **Campaign photograph** → Upload → **Save changes**. Remove it again to go back to the panels.

```text
Premium high-street fashion campaign photograph (H&M / Zara / COS look) for the first screen of a clothing website. A woman and a man in their late twenties standing together, relaxed and natural, in easy everyday clothes in warm neutrals: she wears a fine cream cable-knit cardigan over a white top and straight stone trousers; he wears a heavyweight off-white cotton T-shirt under an open navy overshirt with relaxed taupe trousers. Placed in the right half of the frame, framed from the head to mid-shin. Plain warm beige seamless studio backdrop (#E9E2D6) with a soft shadow on the floor, soft diffused daylight from the left, natural skin tones, true-to-life fabric texture, calm confident expressions. Keep the left half and the lower left of the frame empty, smooth backdrop (a title and buttons are set there). No text, no logo, no watermark, no props. Landscape 16:9, 3200 x 1800 px.
```

**For phones:** the photograph is cropped to a taller shape around its centre, so keep the people close to the middle of the right half rather than at the far right edge.

## The campaign band (further down the home page)

The dark band after "New in" shows one collection (Admin → Settings → Home page → **Campaign band**). It uses the collection's own photograph unless you upload one there.

```text
Premium high-street fashion editorial photograph (H&M / Zara / COS look). A woman in her late twenties walking slowly along a quiet sunlit coastal promenade in the early evening, in a loose linen shirt and wide-leg linen trousers in sand and white, a light knit draped over her shoulders. Warm low golden light, soft long shadows, the sea softly out of focus behind her. Framed from the head to the ankles, true-to-life fabric texture, natural skin tones, calm unposed expression. No text, no logo, no watermark. Portrait 4:5, 2000 x 2500 px.
```
